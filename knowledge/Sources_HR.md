## **Document 2 — Employee Handbook: Compensation & Hiring (excerpt)**

**GreenThreads Employee Handbook · Section 3 — Compensation & Hiring** **Version:** 2026.1

**3.1 Compensation philosophy.** GreenThreads pays hourly retail staff **at or above the local market median** for comparable roles. We believe fair, competitive pay reduces turnover, protects service quality, and reflects our values as an employer. Managers setting pay for a new market should benchmark against local comparable roles before extending an offer.

**3.2 Offers.** Offers are extended in writing and specify rate, status, hours, start date, and any contingencies. Offers should be extended promptly once a candidate is selected; a long gap between application and start date increases the risk that strong candidates accept other offers.

**3.3 Onboarding.** New hourly hires complete a **two-week paid training** before a store opens to the public. Training covers point-of-sale, product and sustainability knowledge, and floor service standards.

**3.4 Turnover and time-to-fill.** People metrics we watch include time-to-fill, offer-accept rate, and 90-day retention. A declining offer-accept rate is an early signal that pay or timing is out of step with the local market.
