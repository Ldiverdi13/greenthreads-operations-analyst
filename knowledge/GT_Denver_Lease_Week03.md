# **Retail Lease Agreement — Summary Terms & Key Provisions**

**LANDLORD:** Cherry Creek Retail Partners, LLC   
**TENANT:** GreenThreads, Inc.   
**PREMISES:** Suite 120, Cherry Creek North, Denver, CO; approx. 2,400 RSF  
**LEASE DATE:** July 10, 2026

---

### **§1. Premises**

Landlord leases to Tenant Suite 120, comprising approximately 2,400 RSF of ground-floor retail space, together with non-exclusive use of common areas. The Premises are leased for use as a retail apparel store and for no other purpose without Landlord’s prior written consent.

### **§2. Term**

The initial term of this Lease is sixty (60) months, commencing on the Rent Commencement Date defined in §4.2 and continuing for five (5) years thereafter, unless sooner terminated in accordance with this Lease. Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew for an additional sixty (60) months at then-prevailing market rent, exercisable by written notice no later than 180 days before expiration.

### **§3. Delivery, Possession & Build-Out**

**§3.1 Delivery Date.** Landlord shall deliver the Premises to Tenant in tenant-ready condition on or about **September 1, 2026**. Landlord represents that it has every expectation of meeting this date and will use commercially reasonable efforts to do so.

**§3.2 Base-Building Work.** Delivery in tenant-ready condition is subject to substantial completion of Landlord’s base-building work, including demising walls, HVAC distribution to the Premises, and a code-compliant electrical panel.

**§3.3 Condition.** Tenant shall accept the Premises in their as-delivered condition. Landlord makes no warranty as to fitness for Tenant’s particular fixturing or merchandising plan.

**§3.4 Tenant Build-Out.** Following delivery of the Premises in tenant-ready condition, Tenant shall perform its own improvements (flooring, fixtures, millwork, signage installation, and final finishes). The parties acknowledge that a build-out of this scope **customarily requires six to eight (6–8) weeks** from the date the Premises are delivered, subject to permitting and inspection by the City of Denver. Tenant is responsible for scheduling its own contractors.

### **§4. Rent**

**§4.1 Base Rent.** Base rent is **$42.00 per RSF per year**, payable in equal monthly installments of **$8,400.00**, due on the first day of each month. Base rent escalates three percent (3%) annually on each anniversary of the Rent Commencement Date.

**§4.2 Rent Commencement Date.** Rent commences on the **earlier of** (a) the date Tenant opens for business to the public, or (b) **sixty (60) days after the Delivery Date** defined in §3.1. Rent accrues from the Rent Commencement Date regardless of whether Tenant has opened.

**§4.3 Additional Rent.** Tenant shall pay its pro-rata share of common area maintenance, property taxes, and insurance (CAM), estimated at $9.50 per RSF per year, reconciled annually.

### **§5. Security Deposit**

Upon execution, Tenant shall deposit **$16,800.00** (equal to two months’ base rent) as security for performance of its obligations. The deposit is refundable, less lawful deductions, within 60 days after expiration.

### **§6. Tenant Improvement Allowance**

Landlord shall provide a tenant improvement allowance of **$60,000.00** ($25.00 per RSF), disbursed against submitted invoices for approved improvements after Tenant opens for business and delivers lien waivers. Any improvement cost above the allowance is Tenant’s sole responsibility.

### **§7. Use, Signage & Assignment**

Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Exterior signage rights are to be addressed under a separate signage rider to be negotiated in good faith; nothing in this Lease grants signage rights absent that rider.

### **§8. Miscellaneous**

This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No penalty, offset, rent abatement, or Landlord liability for delayed delivery of the Premises is provided for in this Lease. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.
