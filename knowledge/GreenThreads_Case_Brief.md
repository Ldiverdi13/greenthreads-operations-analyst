  GreenThreads – Case Brief  
  AI.205  ·  Capstone 1  ·  Summer 2026

Who You Are

**You don’t work for GreenThreads. You’re the consultants they hired.**

Your team is a five-person AI consulting firm. GreenThreads is your client. They have a store to open and nobody to open it with, and they brought you in to find where AI can carry the load.

Your deliverable is not a report. It’s a **working AI-powered analyst** — a designed, documented AI workflow that does a real GreenThreads job. You are not the analyst. You are the team that *builds* the analyst.

This is a real job, and it’s the one to want. Companies call it a **forward deployed engineer**: you go into a business, learn how the work actually happens, and convert it to AI.

The Mandate

*“Denver opens in 90 days. No new headcount.*   
*Show me where AI absorbs the load.”*  **— Marcus, CEO**

**Store \#13. $450,000. Ninety days. Zero new corporate hires.**

One clarification, because it trips everyone: *no new headcount* means no new **corporate** roles — no analysts, no coordinators, nobody at HQ. The Denver store still needs its floor staff hired. That’s the job HR has to pull off with the team they already have.

The Denver Store

A 3,200 sq ft store on a 7-day week, roughly 11 hours a day. That’s about 77 store-hours to cover, with two to four people on the floor at any time. It takes 14 people:

| Role | Headcount | Type | Reports to |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Store Manager** | 1 | Full-time | Regional lead (HQ) |
| **Assistant Manager** | 2 | Full-time | Store Manager |
| **Sales Associate** | 8 | Mostly part-time | Assistant Manager |
| **Stock Associate** | 2 | Part-time | Assistant Manager |
| **Visual Merchandiser** | 1 | Part-time | Store Manager |
| **Total** | 14 |  |  |

Sales Associate is the whole problem: 8 of the 14 seats, the highest turnover, and the hardest to fill fast. **Seven of the 14 are already accepted. Seven are not, and the clock is at 90 days.**

Your Client Contacts

**Marcus — CEO.** Gut-driven, expansion-focused. He set the clock. He’s the audience for your final presentation, and he wants to hear that Denver opens.

**Jennifer — CFO, second-in-command.** Data-driven, cautious. She controls the money, she signs off on your work, and she asks how you know. GreenThreads raised a Series B eighteen months ago, so she answers to a board that expects discipline instead of vibes.

They are not aligned. That’s the engagement. **Your PM is the single point of contact to Jennifer**. All questions, updates, and sign-off all run through your PM. 

The Company

Sustainable apparel — eco-friendly clothing sold in stores and online.

* 12 stores. Denver is \#13. The recent run: Seattle → Portland → Austin.

* Series B, \~$40M revenue, not yet profitable. Growth is funded, but every dollar gets watched.

* Two channels: retail and e-commerce. Four marketing channels: Instagram, Google Ads, email, Facebook.

* Customers are environmentally conscious. Austin’s survey: location 8.7, cleanliness 9.1, inventory selection 7.8, overall experience 8.4.

* Competitors: REI and Patagonia, both deeply established in Denver, plus regional sustainable brands.

The Products

**GreenThreads sells eight SKUs across its 12 stores. Denver launches with four of them.**

| Product | Supplier (country) | Lead | MOQ | Cost | MSRP | Margin |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Classic Tee** | Delta Organics (India) | 45 d | 500 | $14.25 | $38 | 62.5% |
| **Active Shorts** | Mekong Textile Co. (Vietnam) | 60 d | 300 | $22.00 | $58 | 62.1% |
| **Bamboo Joggers** | Song Hong Apparel (Vietnam) | 65 d | 250 | $30.00 | $78 | 61.5% |
| **EcoFleece Hoodie** | Andes Knitworks (Colombia) | 30 d | 200 | $38.00 | $98 | 61.2% |

The other four — Performance Polo, Organic Tank Top, Slim Chino, Recycled Denim Jacket — are in the GreenThreads catalog and stocked at other stores, but they are **not in the Denver launch assortment**. Don’t plan around them, don’t budget for them, don’t market them. They’re in the SKU catalog marked In\_Denver\_Launch \= N so you can see the full catalog your client actually sells.

Four SKUs, not eight, is a deliberate call by your client: fewer SKUs means deeper stock on each one and fewer supplier relationships to manage against a 90-day clock. The full opening buy is $110,000 at cost / $287,800 at retail — all four SKUs, roughly six weeks of stock.

**Read the lead times again.** You have 90 days. The Bamboo Joggers take 65 of them. That order has to be placed within the next 25 days or Denver opens with empty shelves — and that assumes the supplier hits their date. Check whether they do.

Two of the four come from Vietnam, and they are **half the opening inventory buy — $54,900 of $110,000**. There’s an active typhoon-season risk on that lane. Nobody has hedged it.

What AI is Doing at GreenThreads Today

Almost nothing. A few people use ChatGPT unofficially. Nothing is built into any workflow. There’s an IT team but **no data or analytics function** — reporting is ad hoc, and it’s somebody’s Friday afternoon.

There’s also **no AI policy**. Nobody has said what can be fed to a public tool, and GreenThreads holds real customer data and real employee data. That’s not permission. That’s the gap your guardrail closes.
