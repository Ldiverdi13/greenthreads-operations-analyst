## **Document 2 — Inbound Receiving & Inspection SOP**

**GreenThreads Operations · Standard Operating Procedure OPS-04** **Title:** Inbound Shipment Receiving & Inspection **Version:** 2.1 · **Owner:** Operations Manager

**Purpose.** To ensure inbound merchandise arrives on time, is inspected on receipt, and any discrepancy is logged before goods reach the sales floor.

**Scope.** All inbound purchase orders to any GreenThreads store, domestic or imported.

**Procedure.**

1. **Order-by planning.** Before placing any order, confirm the required in-store date and work backward using the supplier’s stated lead time. For **imported** shipments, always account for ocean-freight transit and customs clearance in addition to production lead time.

2. **Supplier reliability buffer.** For any supplier with a documented history of late delivery, Operations must build a **minimum two-week buffer** into the order-by date. A supplier’s promised date on a PO is a planning input, not a guarantee; the buffer protects the opening.

3. **On receipt.** Inspect every carton against the PO for quantity, SKU, and visible damage within 24 hours of arrival. Record actual receipt date.

4. **Discrepancy logging.** Any variance between promised and actual receipt date, or between ordered and received quantity, must be logged in the inbound shipment record the same day and escalated to the Operations Manager if it affects an opening timeline.

5. **Release to floor.** Merchandise may be moved to the sales floor only after inspection is complete and any discrepancy is logged.

**Note.** Late or short shipments are the single most common cause of a missed opening. When a supplier’s promised date leaves no buffer, flag it before the order is placed — not after the shipment is late.

## **Document 3 — Supplier Master Terms (Song Hong Apparel, excerpt)**

**Master Supply Agreement — Song Hong Apparel Co., Ltd. and GreenThreads, Inc.** **Selected Terms · Effective 2025**

**§4. Delivery.** Delivery dates provided by Supplier in any quotation, order acknowledgment, or purchase-order confirmation are **good-faith estimates and are not guarantees.** Supplier will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet confirmed dates.

**§5. Delays.** Supplier shall not be liable for delays arising from production capacity constraints, quality-inspection outcomes, port congestion, ocean-freight scheduling, or customs. Supplier will notify Buyer of a known delay as soon as practicable.

**§6. Remedies.** This Agreement provides no liquidated damages, price reduction, or penalty for late delivery. Buyer’s remedy for a materially late shipment is limited to cancellation of the affected purchase order prior to dispatch.

**§7. Inspection.** All goods are subject to pre-shipment quality inspection at Supplier’s facility. Dispatch will not occur until inspection is passed.

**§9. Quantities.** Supplier will fill confirmed order quantities subject to a tolerance of plus or minus two percent (±2%) on cut-and-sew apparel.
