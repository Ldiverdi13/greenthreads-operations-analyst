## **Document 1 — Paid-Social Campaign Brief**

**GreenThreads Marketing — Denver Launch Paid-Social Brief** **Prepared for:** Channel planning · **Date:** July 2026

**Objective.** Maximize return on Denver launch ad spend across paid social.

**Headline finding.** Instagram is our strongest channel. Across the campaign window, Instagram returned a blended **ROAS of 6.20** — $6.20 in revenue for every $1 spent — the highest of any channel. Facebook, by comparison, returned **3.00**. Google Ads returned 5.10 and Email 4.00.

**Recommendation.** Shift budget out of Facebook and concentrate spend on Instagram for the Denver launch. Instagram is clearly where every marketing dollar works hardest, and scaling it up should carry the launch.

**Supporting note.** These figures are blended across the full campaign period and all spend levels. Instagram carried the largest share of spend ($32,000) and still delivered the top blended return.

## **Document 2 — Channel Performance Report**

**GreenThreads Marketing — Channel Performance Recap (pre-launch test period)**

| Channel | Spend | Revenue | Blended ROAS |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Instagram | $32,000 | $198,400 | 6.20 |
| Google Ads | $24,000 | $122,400 | 5.10 |
| Email | $8,000 | $32,000 | 4.00 |
| Facebook | $16,000 | $48,000 | 3.00 |

**Reading the table.** On a blended basis Instagram leads, followed by Google Ads. Email is efficient on a small budget. Facebook trails on blended return.

**Analyst footnotes.**

* Blended ROAS averages every dollar spent on a channel. It does not show how the **last** dollar performed. Returns on Instagram **soften as daily spend increases** — the channel is strongest at moderate budgets and less efficient when pushed hard.

* Facebook’s blended ROAS is lowest, but Facebook also runs our broad-reach awareness campaigns, which seed conversions later attributed to other channels.

* A blended average can recommend “spend more here” even when the next dollar in that channel would return less than a dollar moved elsewhere. To decide reallocation, look at performance **by spend level**, not the blended number.

## **Document 3 — Denver Launch Media Plan (Draft)**

**GreenThreads Marketing — Denver Launch Media Plan (draft allocation)**

Proposed allocation of the $32,000 paid-social budget for the Denver launch window:

* **Instagram — 75%** ($24,000). Our top blended performer; the plan concentrates spend here to carry launch awareness and conversion.

* **Facebook — 10%** ($3,200). Reduced from prior periods; retained only for retargeting.

* **Google Ads — held separately** under the Search line.

* **Email — house list**, no incremental media cost.

**Rationale.** Put the money where the blended return is highest. Scale Instagram, trim Facebook.

**Open question flagged by the team.** Before we lock this, we should confirm that Instagram’s return holds at the higher daily spend this plan implies. If it softens at scale, a 75% concentration may leave return on the table.
