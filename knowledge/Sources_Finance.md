## **Document 2 — Expense & Procurement Policy**

**GreenThreads Finance · Policy FIN-02** **Title:** Expense Coding & Purchase Approval **Version:** 1.4 · **Owner:** CFO

**Purpose.** To ensure every dollar of pre-opening spend is coded to the correct budget line and approved at the right level, so budget reports reflect reality.

**Coding rules.**

1. **Code to the vendor’s primary service.** Each expense is coded to the budget line that matches what the vendor actually provides — not the project it supports. A construction vendor codes to Buildout even if the work is “for the launch.”

2. **Advertising media.** Purchases of advertising media (Meta, Google, TikTok, and similar platforms) code to **Paid Social** or **Search**, according to the platform. Media buys do **not** code to Launch Event. Launch Event covers the in-store opening event only — venue, catering, printed materials, staffing for the event.

3. **Approval thresholds.** Invoices up to $5,000 may be approved by the function owner. Invoices above $5,000 require CFO approval. Any spend that would push a budget line over its budgeted amount requires CFO approval regardless of size.

**Why it matters.** Miscoded spend makes a line look under- or over-budget when it isn’t. A media buy booked to the wrong line hides real marketing spend and inflates a line that should be nearly untouched. Coding discipline is what lets Finance trust the budget report.
